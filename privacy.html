Privacy policy

Animation Studio · effective 14 October 2026

Animation Studio is a tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Living Mechanisms, on his own computer. This policy says what YouTube data it accesses, what it does with it, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

What it accesses

Only when the owner connects his YouTube account and allows it, through Google’s sign-in:

  • his channel’s name, handle and public statistics, and the list of his uploaded videos (YouTube Data API, read only);
  • his channel’s analytics: views, watch time, audience retention, and the age groups of signed-in viewers as percentages (YouTube Analytics API). These are totals; no individual viewer is identified;
  • permission to upload his videos, set their thumbnails and add their captions (YouTube Data API).

How it is used

Only to upload the episodes the owner approves, private and scheduled for their publish times, and to show him how his channel’s episodes perform. It is not used for advertising, it is never sold, and it is not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where it is kept

The sign-in token and the analytics the tool fetches are stored only on the owner’s computer, in the tool’s local data folder. There is no Animation Studio server: data passes only between that computer and Google. The owner may use an AI assistant on his computer (Claude, by Anthropic) to review the channel’s aggregate analytics. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Removing access and data

Disconnect in the tool’s Settings revokes its access with Google and deletes the token and the stored analytics from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time in Google’s third-party access settings. Videos already uploaded stay on the channel as the owner’s content.

YouTube and Google

Animation Studio uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google holds.

Animation Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new effective date.
